3. Age Restriction and Eligibility

ORIX Revolution is strictly intended for adults aged 18 years and older. The App is not designed for, directed at, or intended to be used by children or anyone under the age of 18.

By creating an account or using the App, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. Individuals under 18 are not permitted to register for or use ORIX Revolution. Parents and guardians should not permit minors under their care to access the App.

We reserve the right to restrict or terminate accounts where we reasonably determine that the user does not meet the minimum age requirement.

If we become aware that a person under 18 has provided personal information to us, we will take appropriate steps to investigate and delete the information where required by applicable law.

If you believe that a minor has registered for an account or provided personal information, contact support@orixrevolution.site.

4. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the version of the App, we may collect or process the following categories of information.

4.1 Account Information

When you register for an ORIX account, we may collect your name or display name, email address, account identification information, referral codes entered during registration, account creation date, and account status.

This information is used to create and manage your account, authenticate access, and provide the App’s features.

4.2 App Activity and Progress

To provide interactive features, we may process mining session status and activity records, task completion records, referral counts and activity, game scores and leaderboard positions, bonus and boost activity, reward-related activity records, and other information required to maintain your progress within the App.

This information helps us maintain records of in-app activities, display user progress, prevent abuse, and improve the user experience. Records of in-app activity do not, by themselves, represent a bank deposit, cash balance, cryptocurrency holding, or guaranteed monetary entitlement.

4.3 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the services and software libraries integrated into the App, certain technical information may be processed, including device or app installation identifiers, app version and operating system information, diagnostic information and crash reports, basic usage information needed for security and functionality, and advertising-related identifiers where supported advertising services are enabled.

We use such information where necessary to maintain app functionality, prevent misuse, diagnose technical problems, and support applicable advertising services. We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location information unless a feature requiring it is introduced with appropriate disclosures and permissions.

4.4 Cryptocurrency and Financial Services

ORIX Revolution does not provide an in-app cryptocurrency exchange, blockchain transaction service, or cryptocurrency withdrawal service. Cryptocurrency-related services or transactions associated with separate websites, platforms, or external services are outside the direct transaction-processing functions of this App.

The App does not require users to provide cryptocurrency wallet private keys or seed phrases as part of its ordinary account registration process. Users should exercise caution when using external services and independently review their privacy policies and terms.

4.5 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless a specific feature is introduced and appropriately disclosed, we do not intentionally collect government-issued identity documents, bank account credentials, payment card numbers, cryptocurrency wallet private keys or seed phrases, contacts or address books, or personal messages from other applications.

The information actually collected may depend on the App’s implementation and its integrated third-party services.

5. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the App to:

  1. Register and maintain user accounts.
  2. Authenticate users and protect account access.
  3. Save user preferences, progress, scores, and task records.
  4. Operate mining-style activities, boosts, referrals, and other interactive features.
  5. Display leaderboards and relevant in-app statistics.
  6. Identify suspicious activity, spam, fraud, or abuse.
  7. Troubleshoot errors and improve app stability.
  8. Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  9. Support advertising services, where applicable.
  10. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We limit our use of information to legitimate purposes connected with operating, securing, and improving the App.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the App, including authentication, cloud hosting, database, diagnostics, and advertising providers. These providers receive or process information as necessary to provide their services.

Certain information, such as display names, scores, referral statistics, or leaderboard positions, may be visible to other users where the relevant feature is enabled. We may disclose relevant information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, investigate abuse, or safeguard the security and integrity of the App.

8. Data Storage and Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect user information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the relevant service, these measures may include authentication and access controls, server-side processing of sensitive operations, security rules governing access to stored information, protected communications between the App and supported backend services, and monitoring and troubleshooting of security-related issues.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information entrusted to us.

Users should protect their account credentials and notify us if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App’s services, maintain account records, address security concerns, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate security needs. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the service through which it is processed.

10. Account Deletion and Data Removal

Users may request deletion of their ORIX Revolution accounts and associated personal information by contacting support@orixrevolution.site.

Please use the subject line ORIX Account Deletion Request and provide sufficient information for us to identify the relevant account. Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or other sensitive authentication secrets.

We will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete the account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate security or fraud-prevention requirements.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law or necessary to resolve disputes, investigate abuse, or maintain security. Where information is retained for these reasons, access and use will be restricted to the relevant purposes.

Deleting the App from a device does not necessarily delete the account or information stored on our servers. Where required by Google Play, we will provide an accessible account-deletion request mechanism outside the App in addition to any required in-app deletion functionality.
