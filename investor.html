ORIX NETWORK INVESTOR PROGRAM

This agreement governs participation in the ORIX Network investor program. The official, approved agreement must be published by ORIX Network administrators and assigned a version number before investors can accept it.

1. Investor Contribution

Each investor's contribution must be verified and recorded in the official investor ledger. A contribution does not automatically guarantee a profit or a return of capital.

2. Revenue Allocation

Eligible revenue, operating expenses, reserves, and the investor allocation percentage must be determined by the approved ORIX agreement. Only verified revenue should be included in investor income calculations.

3. Individual Investor Share

Each investor's percentage, contribution, and accumulated income must match the official investment records and the applicable agreement.

4. Revenue Reporting

Dashboard figures are informational financial records. The source, calculation period, allocation rules, and adjustment history must be available for verification.

5. Risks and Disclosures

Digital assets and investment activities may involve substantial risk. Returns are not guaranteed unless a specific enforceable obligation has been established. Investors should review all legal, financial, and regulatory disclosures before accepting the agreement.

6. Official Agreement Required

This sample text is not the previously prepared ORIX investor agreement. It does not specify the actual agreed percentages and must not be used as a substitute for that document.
