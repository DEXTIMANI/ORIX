Private Investment & Revenue Participation Agreement

For the Development, Operation and AdMob-only Monetization of the Orix Digital Ecosystem.

THIS PRIVATE INVESTMENT AND REVENUE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) takes effect on the date of acceptance and execution recorded by the Developer. BETWEEN: DEXTIMANI DIGITAL SERVICES, a digital technology business operating in Aba, Abia State, Nigeria, hereinafter referred to as the “Developer”; AND EACH INVESTOR ACCEPTED BY THE DEVELOPER under a separately verified contribution and participation record. The Developer and each accepted Investor may individually be referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.” References to Investor 1 and Investor 2 are illustrative labels for investor records maintained by the Developer.

1. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

1.1 The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which Investor 1 and Investor 2 shall provide financial contributions toward the development, deployment, operation, marketing, maintenance and growth of the Orix Digital Ecosystem (“Orix” or the “Project”). 1.2 The Project is being developed and operated by the Developer and is intended to include a mobile application, website and associated digital infrastructure and services. 1.3 The Parties agree that the total initial funding requirement for the Project covered by this Agreement is ₦1,500,000 (One Million Five Hundred Thousand Naira) (“Project Funding Requirement”). 1.4 The Project Funding Requirement is intended to support the development and establishment of Orix, including the website, mobile application, backend infrastructure, technical development, deployment, marketing, operational requirements and other reasonable costs associated with launching and developing the Project. 1.5 In consideration of their respective financial contributions, Investor 1 and Investor 2 shall be entitled to participate in the Investor Revenue Allocation described in this Agreement. 1.6 The Parties expressly agree that the Investors' rights under this Agreement are revenue-participation rights only and do not constitute ownership of the Developer or ownership of the Orix intellectual property unless expressly provided in a separate written agreement.

2. DESCRIPTION OF THE ORIX PROJECT

2.1 Orix is intended to operate as a digital ecosystem incorporating cryptocurrency-related features, digital-wallet functionality, entertainment features, user engagement systems and other digital services. 2.2 The Project may include, but is not limited to: 2.2.1 Website The Project may include an official Orix website providing information, user services, project-related functionality, community features, support and other services determined by the Developer. 2.2.2 Wallet Dashboard The Orix ecosystem may include: a. Web3 wallet connectivity; b. digital-asset and cryptocurrency balance management; c. supported digital-asset conversions; d. USDT and other supported digital-asset functionality; e. deposits and withdrawals where available and legally permissible; f. transfers between supported wallets; g. transaction history; and h. other wallet-related functionality. 2.2.3 Miner's Hub The Project may include: a. timed mining or reward sessions; b. mining-related reward systems; c. daily streaks; d. user engagement rewards; e. 2D mini-games; f. promotional spinning-wheel features; g. missions and activities; h. referral systems; i. progression systems; and j. other features introduced by the Developer. 2.2.4 Community and Leaderboards The Project may include leaderboards and community systems based on activities such as: a. game scores; b. referrals; c. user activity; d. mining achievements; e. balances; f. engagement; and g. other criteria determined by the Developer. 2.3 The Developer shall have the right to modify, improve, replace, suspend or discontinue individual features where reasonably necessary for technical, commercial, security, operational, regulatory or user-experience reasons. 2.4 The description of Project features in this Agreement does not constitute a guarantee that every described feature will remain permanently available.

3. TOTAL PROJECT FUNDING REQUIREMENT

3.1 The Parties acknowledge that the agreed initial Project Funding Requirement is ₦1,500,000. 3.2 The contributions of Investor 1 and Investor 2 shall be recorded against this total Project Funding Requirement. 3.3 Each Investor's Project Participation Percentage shall be determined by dividing that Investor's qualifying contribution by ₦1,500,000. 3.4 The formula shall be: Investor Project Participation Percentage = Investor Contribution ÷ ₦1,500,000 × 100 3.5 For example, an Investor contributing ₦500,000 shall have: ₦500,000 ÷ ₦1,500,000 × 100 = 33.33% 3.6 An Investor contributing ₦750,000 shall have 50%. 3.7 An Investor contributing ₦250,000 shall have 16.67%. 3.8 The Parties shall record the actual contribution and corresponding percentage for each Investor in Schedule 1.

4. INVESTOR CONTRIBUTIONS

INVESTOR 1 Contribution: ₦__________________________________________ Percentage of Project Funding Requirement: _________________% INVESTOR 2 Contribution: ₦__________________________________________ Percentage of Project Funding Requirement: _________________% 4.1 Each contribution shall be paid through a payment method agreed upon by the Parties. 4.2 A contribution shall become a qualifying contribution once the Developer has received and verified the funds. 4.3 The Developer shall acknowledge receipt of each Investor's contribution. 4.4 The Investors' respective percentages shall be based on the actual amounts received and accepted by the Developer. 4.5 Minimum amount allowed for investment is ₦200,000, and the maximum is ₦500,000.

5. INVESTOR REVENUE ALLOCATION

5.1 The Parties agree that the Net Revenue generated by the Orix Project shall be divided into two principal allocations: 50% — Developer Allocation 50% — Investor Revenue Allocation 5.2 The Developer shall retain the Developer Allocation. 5.3 The Investor Revenue Allocation shall be distributed between Investor 1 and Investor 2 according to their respective Project Participation Percentages. 5.4 An Investor's entitlement is calculated as: Investor Distribution = Net Revenue × 50% × Investor's Project Participation Percentage 5.5 The Investor's Project Participation Percentage applies to the 50% Investor Revenue Allocation, not to 100% of Net Revenue.

6. EXAMPLE OF INVESTOR REVENUE CALCULATION

6.1 The following example is included to make the Parties' agreed calculation method clear. 6.2 Assume: Total Project Funding Requirement = ₦1,500,000 Investor Contribution = ₦500,000 Investor Project Participation Percentage = 33.33% 6.3 If the Project generates ₦4,000,000 in Net Revenue during a particular month: 50% Developer Allocation = ₦2,000,000 50% Investor Revenue Allocation = ₦2,000,000 The Investor's entitlement shall be: 33.33% × ₦2,000,000 = approximately ₦666,667 6.4 Accordingly, an Investor contributing ₦500,000 toward the ₦1,500,000 Project Funding Requirement would receive approximately ₦666,667 where the applicable Net Revenue is ₦4,000,000. 6.5 The example is illustrative and does not constitute a guarantee that the Project will generate ₦4,000,000 or any other specific amount of revenue.

7. DEVELOPER ALLOCATION

7.1 Fifty percent (50%) of Net Revenue shall constitute the Developer Allocation. 7.2 The Developer Allocation shall belong to and be controlled by the Developer for the continued development, operation, maintenance, improvement and growth of Orix. 7.3 The Developer may use the Developer Allocation for legitimate Project-related purposes, including but not limited to: a. website development and maintenance; b. mobile application development; c. software development; d. server and cloud infrastructure; e. Firebase and database services; f. hosting; g. cybersecurity; h. technical support; i. bug fixing; j. application maintenance; k. UI/UX improvements; l. new features; m. marketing and advertising; n. user acquisition; o. promotional campaigns; p. community management; q. customer support; r. third-party APIs and services; s. software licences; t. legal and professional expenses; u. accounting and administrative expenses; v. compliance and regulatory expenses; w. operational expenses; and x. other reasonable expenses determined by the Developer to be necessary or beneficial to the Project. 7.4 The Developer shall have full discretion over the use and management of the Developer Allocation. 7.5 The Investors shall have no ownership, control, approval right or management authority over the Developer Allocation. 7.6 The Investors shall not be entitled to demand a detailed breakdown, receipt-by-receipt accounting, invoice disclosure or other internal accounting of how the Developer Allocation is spent, provided that the Developer calculates the Investor Revenue Allocation in accordance with this Agreement. 7.7 The Developer's internal use of the Developer Allocation shall not alter the Investors' agreed percentage of the Investor Revenue Allocation.

8. NET REVENUE

8.1 For purposes of calculating the Investor Revenue Allocation, “Net Revenue” or “Net AdMob Revenue” means Google AdMob revenue attributable to Orix that has actually been paid and cleared to the Developer, after deduction of applicable transaction charges, payment-processing charges, platform commissions, refunds, chargebacks, applicable taxes and other directly attributable deductions necessary to determine the revenue actually available for allocation. 8.2 For Investor distributions under this Agreement, the sole qualifying revenue source is Google AdMob revenue attributable to the Orix Project and actually paid by Google to the Developer. Revenue from subscriptions, product sales, token transactions, sponsorships, partnerships, other advertising networks, in-app purchases or any other source is excluded unless the Parties amend this Agreement in a separate written agreement signed by the Developer and the affected Investor. 8.3 “Received” means Google has completed payment and the relevant AdMob funds have cleared into an account controlled by the Developer. Estimated earnings shown in the AdMob dashboard, unpaid balances, payment-on-hold amounts, invalid-traffic adjustments, pending payments and projected revenue are not received funds and are not available for distribution or withdrawal. 8.4 Investor distributions shall be calculated only after the Developer verifies the actual AdMob payment, deducts applicable taxes, bank charges, currency-conversion costs, payment charges, refunds, chargebacks, invalid-traffic adjustments and other directly attributable deductions, and determines the resulting Net AdMob Revenue. 8.5 No Investor may demand, withdraw, transfer or receive an AdMob-based distribution before the corresponding funds have actually been paid by Google, cleared, and become available to the Developer. Payment timing may depend on Google's payment cycle, account verification, thresholds, policy reviews, adjustments or holds. 8.6 No distribution is payable for a period in which no qualifying Net AdMob Revenue has been received.
